# PSX Firearms Free

**A free PS1-style pistol and pump shotgun for your first-person game**, whose parts move: the pistol's slide kicks back and its magazine drops out, the shotgun's pump racks. With their ammo, a spare magazine, a shell and a steel target that swings when hit, and a firing range in the demo to shoot them in. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per prop with its colliders, ready to drop in. Free for commercial use.

### Want more?

**[PSX Firearms](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-firearms)** has 14 guns and 33 animations: a
.45, a revolver whose cylinder swings out, a magnum, a flare gun, a double-barrel and a sawn-off
that break open, a bolt-action and a lever-action rifle, an assault rifle, an SMG, a machine
pistol, a grenade launcher, ammo for all of them, a hand grenade, a crate and a gun case, and a
Unity package. Same addon, same look: it installs over this one.

The ammo boxes and the shell are [PSX Horror Props](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-props)'
own; its [free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-props-free) has 12 props for the
save room, free too.

### What's inside

- **A 9mm pistol** that fires (slide, hammer, trigger) and reloads (the magazine drops out and a
  fresh one goes in), and its **magazine**.
- **A pump shotgun** that fires and pumps, and a **shotgun shell**.
- **9mm and shotgun ammo boxes** and a **steel target** that swings when hit.
- **5 animations** on the moving parts, and **7 inventory icons**, 256x256 PNGs with
  transparency.
- 12 to 272 triangles a prop, one 512x512 texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours,
  real-world scale. props.json has where each gun's muzzle and sights are, its ammo and rounds.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per prop: box colliders on every part (the moving ones move with them), an
AnimationPlayer where it moves, one lit material, and a gallery with the range. Tested on Godot
4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# pistol: the gun in your player's hand
pistol.get_node("AnimationPlayer").play("reload")
```

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns the guns (and your
level) over in one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.

For their sound, [PSX Horror SFX Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-sfx-free) has this
pistol's shot and reload and this pump shotgun's shot and pump, free under CC BY; [PSX Horror
SFX](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-sfx) has all 14 guns.

For the screens around them, [PSX Horror UI](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-ui) is a PS1
survival horror menu kit for Godot 4 (inventory, examine, map, files, typewriter save) whose item
icons are rendered from PSX Firearms.
